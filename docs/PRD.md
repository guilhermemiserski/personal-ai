# AI Personal Trainer Web App – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision

Build an AI-powered web application that acts as a **personal trainer**, generating personalized workout plans and continuously adapting them based on the user’s performance, progress, goals, and feedback.

The platform should feel like having a real personal trainer that monitors progress, adjusts workouts, tracks performance, and gives recommendations over time.

The app should be modern, intuitive, mobile-friendly, and highly personalized.

---

## 2. Main Goal

Allow users to:

1. Create a profile with body and training information.
2. Receive a personalized training plan generated by AI.
3. Log workouts and progress.
4. Receive dynamic adaptations to their training based on evolution.
5. Get continuous AI coaching and recommendations.

---

## 3. Core Features

### 3.1 User Onboarding

When a user signs up, they must complete a fitness assessment.

#### Required Inputs

**Personal Information**

- Age
- Biological sex
- Height
- Weight

**Fitness Information**

- Training experience:
  - Never trained
  - Beginner (0–6 months)
  - Intermediate (6 months–2 years)
  - Advanced (2+ years)

**Goal Selection** — one primary goal:

- Weight loss
- Muscle gain (hypertrophy)
- Strength gain
- Body recomposition
- General health
- Athletic performance
- Endurance

**Training Availability**

- Days per week available for training
- Average time available per workout: 30 / 45 / 60 / 90+ min

**Gym Access**

- Full gym access
- Home gym
- Bodyweight only
- Limited equipment

**Injuries / Limitations** (optional)

- Shoulder injury, knee pain, lower back pain, mobility restriction, free text

**Preferred Training Style** (optional)

- Bodybuilding, functional, powerlifting, cross training, mixed

**Fitness Level Questions**

- Can perform pushups?
- Can squat properly?
- Current cardio level
- Current strength level

### 3.2 AI Workout Generation

Personalized plan from onboarding inputs.

**AI must consider:** goal, experience, training days, limitations, duration, equipment, recovery, progressive overload.

**Weekly split examples:** 3d Full Body A/B/C; 4d Upper/Lower; 5d PPL+UL; 6d PPL repeat.

**Per exercise:** name, muscle group, sets, reps, rest, tempo, intensity (RPE), instructions, video, alternatives.

### 3.3 AI Adaptive Coaching

Post-workout log: completed?, RPE effort 1–10, energy, soreness, difficulty, weight progression.

Adapt: too easy → load/volume/intensity up; too hard → reduce fatigue/volume, add recovery; plateau → exercise swap, rep range, deload.

### 3.4 Progress Tracking

Body metrics (weight, BF%, measurements, photos, strength). Training metrics (completed, adherence %, volume, PRs, consistency). Charts.

### 3.5 AI Coach Chat

Contextual answers using history, logs, goals, trends.

### 3.6 Smart Notifications

Motivation, recovery warnings, missed session prompts with adaptation offer.

## 5. AI Logic Requirements

Evidence-based: progressive overload, fatigue management, recovery, volume landmarks, hypertrophy/strength programming.

**Personalization inputs:** goal, experience, recovery, fatigue, adherence, injuries, equipment, time, performance trends.

**Training memory:** past workouts, failures, skips, injuries, PRs, exercise preferences.

## 8. UX Requirements

Premium, modern, fitness-focused, mobile-first, dark mode, charts, smooth animations, low-friction logging. Inspiration: MyFitnessPal, Fitbod, Strong, Hevy, Nike Training Club.

---

## 9. Future Features

Nutrition, wearables, recovery score, voice coach, CV form analysis.

---

## 10. Success Criteria

Consistent training, improved adherence, perceived personalization, intelligent plan evolution, retention via coaching.

---

## Implementation Rule

**Every AI-generated workout must be personalized and evolve from performance + feedback history — never generic templates without user context.**
